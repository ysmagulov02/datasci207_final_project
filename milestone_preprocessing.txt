Data preprocessing, feature selection, and EDA

The EIA's 2018 CBECS public CSV contains 6,436 buildings and 1,249 columns.
MFBTU is annual major-fuels consumption in thousands of Btu (kBtu). Excluding
79 inapplicable targets leaves 6,357 buildings, split into 4,449 training,
954 validation, and 954 test examples (seed 42).

We screened 23 building, operating, equipment, and weather candidates. OCCUPYP
was excluded because 71.7% of training values were missing. Spearman correlations
assessed numeric associations; mutual information also assessed categorical
associations. A four-fold training-only ExtraTrees screening model measured
the increase in held-out-fold MAE after each source variable was shuffled
three times. The screening model used log1p(MFBTU); errors were measured in kBtu.

We retained the ten largest positive mean effects that were positive in at
least three folds: SQFT, PBA, NFLOOR, NWKER, WKHRS, RFTILT, MAINHT, MAINCL, GLSSPC, MONUSE. Area, activity,
floors, staffing, hours, roof tilt, and equipment types had the strongest
support. Glass coverage and months in use had much smaller effects and remain
tentative. Climate-zone effects were unstable across folds. These exploratory
scores depend on the screening model and correlated predictors; they do not
establish causation, the best subset, or final model accuracy. Validation and
test data were not used for selection.

The ten source variables produce 91 encoded predictors. Training medians,
one-hot categories, and optional numeric standardization are fitted on training
rows only. Floor-count codes 994/995 are categories, not literal floor counts.
Equipment skips remain explicit categories. Training IQR fences flag unusual
numeric values; no outliers are removed. Delivered MFBTU values remain unchanged.
Consumption components, bills, IDs, weights, and source flags are excluded from X.

EIA's supplied imputations and masking are retained. Targets include 4,512 reported,
1,637 imputed, and 208 strip-center estimates. Source flags and weights are saved
separately for sensitivity checks and weighted evaluation. Equipment information
must be available to the intended user. Final model evaluation remains separate.

EDA uses the 4,449 training buildings only, without survey weighting. A compact
figure shows the consumption distribution, floor area versus consumption,
box plots for the six most common building activities, and numeric correlations.
Consumption is right-skewed (mean 15.9 million; median 4.4 million kBtu). Area
and employees correlate strongly with consumption (0.91 and 0.84); building-type
differences are unadjusted for size and schedules. These are associations, not
causal effects. Plot log scales do not change the delivered target.
